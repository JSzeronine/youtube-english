The Voice Under My Bed — Ep. 2
I looked at the boy next to my bed.

He looked exactly like me.

He was wearing my blue pajamas.

“Who are you?” I asked.

The boy smiled at me.

“I am you,” he said.

“No, you are not me!” I shouted.

Then my mom came into the room.

“What happened?” she asked.

I pointed at the boy.

But the boy was gone.

My mom looked at me with a worried face.

“There is no one here,” she said.

I looked around my room.

Then I heard a small sound.

“Tap, tap, tap.”

The sound came from my closet.

I slowly opened the closet door.

There was nothing inside.

Suddenly, I heard the voice again.

“Look under your bed.”

I did not want to look.

But my mom looked under the bed first.

She screamed when she saw something there.

It was a picture of me, but I was sleeping under my bed.
