The Voice Under My Bed
It was late at night, and I was lying in my bed.

My parents were sleeping in the next room.

I was reading a book when I heard a small voice.

“Are you still awake?”

I looked around my room, but no one was there.

Then I heard the voice again.

“I am under your bed.”

I stopped breathing for a moment.

I wanted to call my mom, but I was too scared to move.

Then something touched my foot.

I quickly pulled my feet onto the bed.

“Please don't look under the bed,” the voice said.

That made me even more curious.

I slowly got out of bed and looked down.

At first, I saw only darkness.

Then I saw two small eyes looking back at me.

I screamed and ran to my parents' room.

My dad turned on the light and came with me.

We looked under the bed together.

There was nothing there.

My dad smiled and told me it was just a dream.

I went back to bed and tried to sleep.

Then I heard a voice right next to my ear.

“I told you not to look under the bed.”

I slowly turned around, and I saw myself standing beside my bed.
